TASK 3: DATA VISUALIZATION

Project: Books Data Analysis
Dataset: books_data.csv
Total Records: 100
Tools Used: Python, Pandas, Matplotlib


1. Book Rating Distribution
The dataset contains books across all five rating categories.
Three-star and one-star books have the highest counts, with 22 books each.

2. Book Price Distribution
Book prices range from £10.16 to £58.11.
The average book price is approximately £34.56.
Most books are distributed across the middle price ranges.

3. Average Book Price by Rating
Three-star books have the highest average price (£36.84).
Five-star books have the lowest average price (£30.01).

4. Book Rating vs Price
A box plot was used to compare price distributions across all five rating categories.
It displays the median, quartiles, and price variation for each rating.

5. Top 10 Most Expensive Books
A horizontal bar chart identifies the ten most expensive books in the dataset.
The highest-priced book costs £58.11.

6. Project Limitations

- The analysis uses only the first five pages of the website.
- The dataset contains 100 books, so it may not represent the entire website.
- All 100 books were marked as in stock, so availability comparisons were not possible.
- The relationship between book ratings and prices does not prove that ratings influence prices.
